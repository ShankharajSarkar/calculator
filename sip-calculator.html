How Goal-Seek & Step-Up SIP Accelerate Financial Freedom

A Goal-Seek SIP determines the exact monthly savings necessary to reach a specific financial target (such as ₹1 Crore or ₹5 Crore) over a chosen investment horizon.

Why Annual Step-Up Matters

Increasing your SIP amount by just 10% annually can nearly double your final corpus compared to a flat SIP, neutralizing the eroding effect of inflation.
