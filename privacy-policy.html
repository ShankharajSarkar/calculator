Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Kitna Paisa (kitnapaisa.in). We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how information is handled when you use our financial calculation and planning tools.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Kitna Paisa processes your calculations entirely inside your web browser (client-side). We do not record, store, or transmit your financial inputs, salary data, or calculation results to any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identification details (such as names, phone numbers, or bank account details). Any numeric inputs entered into our calculators remain on your local device and are discarded when you refresh or close the tab.

2. Automatically Collected Technical Data & Analytics

When you visit Kitna Paisa, standard non-personally identifiable technical information is automatically collected through web logs and analytics services (such as Google Analytics 4). This includes:

  • Browser type, device version, and operating system.
  • Referring URLs and pages visited on our site.
  • Approximate geographical location (country and city level).
  • Session duration, bounce rates, and basic UI interaction events.

This data is utilized solely to analyze performance, optimize device responsiveness, and improve tool usability.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

Kitna Paisa uses cookies to personalize content and analyze traffic. We partner with third-party advertising platforms, including Google AdSense, to serve relevant advertisements:

  • Google and its partner networks use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet.
  • These cookies allow advertisers to serve targeted advertisements based on browsing preferences without tracking personally identifying details like your name, email address, or phone number.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

4. Financial Advice Disclaimer

All calculations, estimates, interest figures, and tax projections generated by Kitna Paisa are designed for educational, informational, and exploratory purposes only. They do not constitute formal financial, investment, accounting, or legal advice. Users should consult a qualified financial advisor or chartered accountant before making real-world financial commitments.

5. External Links

Our website may contain outbound links to other web resources or official portals. We hold no control over the privacy policies, content, or practices of third-party websites.
