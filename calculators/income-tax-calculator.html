How your tax is calculated

Methodology & regulatory notes

Gross total income = Salary + House Property + Business/Profession + Other Sources + Capital Gains
Old regime taxable income = GTI − HRA exemption − standard deduction − eligible old-regime deductions
New regime taxable income = GTI − standard deduction − eligible 80CCD(2)
Final tax = normal-rate tax + special-rate tax − eligible rebate/relief + surcharge + 4% cess
  • New-regime slab rates used in this model: ₹0–4L Nil; ₹4–8L 5%; ₹8–12L 10%; ₹12–16L 15%; ₹16–20L 20%; ₹20–24L 25%; above ₹24L 30%.
  • Resident-individual 87A rebate is modeled up to ₹60,000 where taxable income is within the ₹12 lakh threshold; special-rate and marginal-relief cases need statutory Schedule SI treatment.
  • HRA is modeled only for the old regime. HRA salary base uses Basic + DA specifically entered as forming part of retirement benefits. The 50%-city list now covers 8 cities from FY 2026-27 (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) — for FY 2025-26 returns, only the original 4 metros qualified.
  • Employer NPS under 80CCD(2) is capped using the employer category and salary base entered; new-regime eligibility is separately modeled.
  • For let-out property, 30% standard deduction is applied to net annual value. Self-occupied interest is not allowed in the new regime and is capped at ₹2 lakh in the old regime in this model.
  • 80C is capped at ₹1.5 lakh, 80CCD(1B) at ₹50,000, and 80DD/80U at ₹75,000 (normal disability) or ₹1,25,000 (severe disability, 80%+) via the dropdown. 80D inputs are capped at common statutory limits based on age selections only where applicable; 80G should be entered as the eligible deduction, not the donation amount.
  • STCG u/s 111A is modeled at a flat 20%. All other short-term capital gains ("Other STCG") are added to normal income and taxed at your slab rate — they are not a flat-rate category. LTCG u/s 112A and other long-term capital gains are both modeled at 12.5% after the ₹1.25 lakh annual 112A exemption, reflecting the 2024 unification of LTCG rates across most asset classes.
  • Surcharge, marginal relief, special-rate surcharge restrictions and rebate interactions can be complex, especially above ₹1 crore where multiple surcharge thresholds interact. This is a planning tool, not ITR filing software — verify high-income and unusual cases against the latest Income Tax Department material.

Is HRA available in the new regime?▼
No. The Income Tax Department states that the section 10(13A) HRA exemption is not available under the new regime.
Can self-occupied home-loan interest be claimed in the new regime?▼
This calculator does not claim it under the new regime. The department's current guidance identifies let-out property interest as the available new-regime house-property deduction.
Does the calculator support TDS?▼
Yes. TDS/TCS and advance/self-assessment tax are deducted from the computed tax to show an estimated balance payable or refund.
Is 'Other STCG' taxed at a flat rate like equity STCG?▼
No. Only STCG under Section 111A (on equity shares and equity mutual funds sold on a recognized exchange with STT paid) is taxed at a flat 20% rate. All other short-term capital gains, such as on debt funds, property, or gold held short-term, are added to your normal income and taxed at your regular slab rate, exactly as this calculator's field label describes.
