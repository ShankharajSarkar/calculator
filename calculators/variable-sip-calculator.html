Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Variable or Step-Up SIP?
A Variable or Step-Up SIP allows investors to increase their recurring monthly contribution at regular intervals (typically once a year). This mirrors annual salary increments or business revenue growth, compounding wealth substantially faster than a fixed-value SIP.
How does inflation alter the purchasing power of my corpus?
Inflation gradually decreases how much a unit of currency can purchase. A ₹1 Crore corpus collected 15 years from now at 6% annual inflation carries the real purchasing power equivalent of approximately ₹41.7 Lakh today. The Inflation-Adjusted Value metrics show your true real purchasing capacity.
How is Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax calculated?
Under current Indian tax provisions (Section 112A), equity mutual fund units redeemed after 12 months are subject to a 12.5% LTCG tax on cumulative profits that exceed the statutory exemption limit of ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year.
What is the difference between Annuity Due and Ordinary Annuity?
Beginning of Month (Annuity Due) assumes your installment is invested on the 1st of each month, earning compounding returns for that month. End of Month (Arrears) deposits cash on the final day, earning interest starting the subsequent period.
