How the Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS) Works

The Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS) is a government-backed fixed-income savings scheme administered through India Post branches. It provides a predictable, steady stream of guaranteed monthly income while keeping the core principal fully protected.

Statutory Investment Limits

  • Single Account: Minimum deposit of ₹1,000 up to a maximum of ₹9,00,000 (₹9 Lakh).
  • Joint Account (Up to 3 Adults): Maximum total investment of ₹15,00,000 (₹15 Lakh), with all holders having an equal share.
  • Multiple Accounts: An individual can hold multiple POMIS accounts provided their cumulative share across all single and joint accounts does not exceed ₹9 Lakh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current POMIS interest rate? ▼
The POMIS rate is 7.4% per annum for the July-September 2026 quarter, unchanged from the previous quarter. The Finance Ministry reviews all small savings scheme rates every quarter, so check the latest notification for anything beyond the current quarter.
What are the maximum investment limits in a POMIS account? ▼
Under official India Post rules, an individual can invest a maximum of ₹9 lakh in a single account, and up to ₹15 lakh in a joint account (held jointly by up to 3 adults with equal share).
What are the premature closure penalty rules? ▼
No withdrawal is permitted within the first 1 year. If closed between 1 and 3 years, a 2% deduction is levied on the principal deposit. If closed between 3 and 5 years, a 1% deduction is applied.
Is interest earned in POMIS taxable? ▼
Yes, monthly interest payouts from POMIS are treated as taxable income and added to your total income under 'Income from Other Sources'. However, no TDS is deducted at source by the post office.
