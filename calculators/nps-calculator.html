How the National Pension System (NPS) Works

The National Pension System (NPS) is a government-sponsored, voluntary, long-term retirement savings scheme managed by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). It allows you to invest systematically into a diversified mix of Equity (E), Corporate Debt (C), and Government Securities (G) across your working life.

NPS Maturity & Annuity Rules

Upon reaching age 60 (superannuation):

  • Lump-Sum Withdrawal (Up to 60%): You can withdraw up to 60% of your total corpus completely tax-free under Section 10(12A).
  • Annuity Purchase (Minimum 40%): At least 40% of the corpus must be used to purchase an annuity plan from an IRDAI-approved Annuity Service Provider (ASP) to guarantee a regular monthly pension for life.
  • Corpus Below ₹8 Lakh: If your accumulated wealth is ₹8 Lakh or less at retirement, you are exempt from mandatory annuity purchase and can withdraw 100% tax-free.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the extra ₹50,000 tax deduction under Section 80CCD(1B)? ▼
Section 80CCD(1B) provides an exclusive tax deduction of up to ₹50,000 for voluntary contributions to an NPS Tier 1 account. This deduction is over and above the ₹1.5 Lakh limit available under Section 80C/80CCD(1), allowing total tax deductions of up to ₹2 Lakh per financial year under the Old Tax Regime.
Can I defer my NPS withdrawal beyond 60 years of age? ▼
Yes. NPS subscribers can choose to defer their lump-sum withdrawal and annuity purchase, staying invested up to the age of 75 or 85 years, while continuing to earn market-linked compounding returns.
What happens in the event of the subscriber's demise before retirement? ▼
In the unfortunate event of the subscriber's demise, 100% of the accumulated NPS corpus is paid directly to the registered nominee or legal heir as a tax-free lump sum.
What is the difference between NPS Tier 1 and Tier 2 accounts? ▼
NPS Tier 1 is the primary retirement account with lock-in until age 60 and exclusive tax deduction benefits. Tier 2 is an optional, fully liquid savings account with zero withdrawal restrictions, but does not offer tax deductions for non-government subscribers.
