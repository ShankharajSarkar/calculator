📐 GST Calculation Methodology

Taxable Value = (Quantity × Rate) − Discount
GST Amount = Taxable Value × (GST Rate / 100)
Intra-State: CGST = GST Amount / 2 | SGST = GST Amount / 2
Inter-State: IGST = GST Amount
Grand Total = Taxable Value + Total GST (Rounded to nearest Rupee)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GST calculated on an invoice? ▼
GST is calculated by applying the applicable GST rate (e.g., 18%) to the taxable value of the goods or services. If the supply is intra-state, the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. If it is inter-state, it is charged as IGST.
What are the mandatory fields in a GST invoice? ▼
A valid GST invoice must include the supplier's name, address, and GSTIN; a consecutive invoice number; date of issue; buyer's name and GSTIN (if registered); HSN/SAC codes; taxable value; and a clear breakdown of CGST, SGST, and IGST amounts.
Is this invoice format compliant with Indian GST laws? ▼
Yes, this tool generates invoices based on Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, incorporating all mandatory fields required for B2B and B2C transactions in India, updated for the latest FY 2026-27 guidelines.
