How Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) Works

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a government of India backed small-deposit savings scheme launched under the Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao initiative. It is designed to help parents build a dedicated, high-interest corpus for their daughter's higher education and marriage.

The 15 vs. 21 Year Compounding Rule

Many investors mistakenly assume deposits are required for 21 years. Under official SSY rules:

  • Deposit Phase (Years 1 to 15): You deposit your chosen amount annually for the first 15 years.
  • Growth Phase (Years 16 to 21): No deposits are allowed or required. The entire accumulated balance continues to compound at the prevailing government interest rate until completing 21 years from account opening.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is eligible to open an SSY account? ▼
Parents or legal guardians can open an SSY account for a resident Indian girl child from her birth up to 10 years of age. A family can open a maximum of 2 SSY accounts (3 in case of twin/triplet girls in first/second birth).
What is the rule for 50% partial withdrawal? ▼
To support higher education, a partial withdrawal of up to 50% of the balance at the end of the previous financial year is permitted once the girl child turns 18 or passes Class 10 (with admission proof).
Can the account be closed before 21 years if the daughter gets married? ▼
Yes. If the girl child gets married after attaining the age of 18 years, the SSY account can be closed prematurely upon submission of an age proof affidavit and wedding documentation.
