How Cab & Bike Taxi Driver Earnings Actually Work in India

Driving on platforms like Uber, Ola, Rapido, and Namma Yatri provides instant daily payouts, but many driver-partners miscalculate their real net profit by focusing solely on gross fares without factoring in fuel inflation, dead kilometers, vehicle depreciation, and platform deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my real hourly wage lower than my app earnings?▼
Your driver partner app only reports gross collections minus platform commissions. It does not subtract the money spent daily on petrol/CNG/electricity, wear and tear on tyres/engine oil, and vehicle loan interest.
What are dead kilometers (dry runs) in ride-hailing?▼
Dead kilometers are the distance driven without a paying passenger — such as driving to pickup points, cruising for ride requests, or returning home after a drop. This fuel burn significantly reduces net hourly profit and this calculator lets you enter it separately from your paid, productive kilometers.
How much difference does an Electric Vehicle (EV) make to driver profits?▼
For cabs driving 150+ km daily, EV running costs are typically cheaper per km than Petrol/Diesel and somewhat cheaper than CNG, though the exact saving depends heavily on whether you charge at home (cheaper) or at public fast-charging stations (often 2x the cost).
Do ride-hailing drivers need to pay income tax on their earnings?▼
Yes. Most driver-partners are treated as self-employed and can opt for presumptive taxation under Section 44AD, where 8% (or 6% for digital receipts) of gross turnover is deemed taxable profit, rather than tracking every expense separately. Very high-turnover drivers registered as e-commerce participants on aggregator platforms should also check GST registration requirements with a tax professional.
