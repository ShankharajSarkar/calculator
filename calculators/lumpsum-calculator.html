How Lumpsum Compounding Builds Wealth

When you invest a single lumpsum amount into equity mutual funds, index funds, or equities, your money works with continuous compounding from day one without installment lags.

Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) Calculation

Under Indian tax regulations, mutual fund equity gains realized after 1 year are eligible for a ₹1.25 Lakh tax-free limit per financial year. Net profits beyond this threshold are taxed at 12.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does lumpsum compounding work? ▼
In a lumpsum deposit, the entire capital earns returns starting on day one. Every year, returns from previous years generate additional interest, creating rapid exponential wealth growth over long horizons.
How is LTCG tax applied on mutual fund lumpsum investments in India? ▼
For Equity Mutual Funds, capital gains realized after 1 year are taxed at 12.5% on amounts exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year.
When should I choose Lumpsum over SIP? ▼
Lumpsum investments yield optimal results during market downturns/corrections or when you receive a lump sum such as an annual bonus, property liquidation, or maturing deposit.
How does inflation impact my purchasing power? ▼
Inflation erodes future buying power. With inflation adjustment turned on, the calculator converts your future corpus into today's equivalent rupee value.
